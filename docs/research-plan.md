# RegisterWatch-Like Regulatory Intelligence Product
# Complete R&D Research Plan & Idea Workbook

## Purpose of this document

This is the **R&D master plan** for researching and designing a regulatory-intelligence website/API similar to RegisterWatch.

The current job is **not coding**.

The current job is to understand:

- What already exists
- Who the competitors are
- What data they provide
- How their websites work
- What their UI/UX does well
- What their UI/UX does poorly
- What information is missing
- What customers actually need
- What regulatory information exists
- Where that information comes from
- How jurisdictions differ
- How operators, licences, brands and domains are connected
- How regulatory information changes
- What grey areas exist
- What information could become a better product
- What could eventually be sold through a website/API

The final objective is:

> **Build a product that solves the problems existing regulatory-intelligence products do not solve well.**

---

# HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Do the research **one step at a time**.

For every step:

1. Read what the step is trying to discover.
2. Search competitors / Google where instructed.
3. Visit websites manually where instructed.
4. Record findings.
5. Capture screenshots when UI/UX is relevant.
6. Save useful URLs.
7. Note missing information.
8. Note ideas for our future product.
9. Do not jump too far ahead unless the research reveals something important.

---

# THE CORE QUESTION THROUGHOUT THE ENTIRE R&D

At every step, keep asking:

> **"What does the customer need to know that is currently difficult, fragmented, expensive, outdated, or unclear?"**

Then ask:

> **"Can we collect authoritative information that answers that question better than existing products?"**

And finally:

> **"Can we present that information in a way that is significantly easier and more useful than what exists today?"**

That is the central R&D objective.

---

# IMPORTANT

Do not start by trying to research everything simultaneously.

The recommended immediate order is:

### FIRST
**Competitor research**

Understand the existing market.

### SECOND
**Competitor UI/UX**

Actually use their products and identify good/bad experiences.

### THIRD
**Competitor data gaps**

Determine what information they have and what they don't.

### FOURTH
**Customer problems**

Understand why customers need this information.

### FIFTH
**Jurisdiction research**

Only then begin the deep jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction regulatory research.

This prevents us from spending months researching information that competitors already commoditize while missing the areas where the real opportunity exists.
